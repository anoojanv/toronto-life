💸 The cheap-ticket playbook

Tuesday & Wednesday Jays games are the cheapest in baseball's second city market — 500-level often under $15 on resale, and you can walk down after the 3rd inning.
Buy day-of, after 4pm. Resale prices on StubHub / SeatGeek / Gametime collapse on weekday afternoons when sellers panic.
Raptors 905 & Marlies are the underrated move: pro-adjacent basketball and hockey for the price of two beers, and the arena bars are never a line.
Leafs weekday tip: standing-room and 300-level singles against non-rival teams (think Columbus on a Tuesday) are the only affordable Leafs tickets that exist.
TFC + BMO Field south-end supporters section is loud, cheap, and weekday matches regularly dip under $20.
Student / under-30? Watch for Jays "Value Games" pricing tiers and Raptors mobile-only drops the morning of the game.

🧭 Weeknight rules of the city

Thursday is the new Friday. King West rooms run Thursday programming that rivals weekends, at half the cover and a third of the line.
Ossington is a bar crawl, not a club crawl. Start north of Queen and walk south; the good rooms announce DJs on Instagram same-day.
Yorkville is a dress-code zone. No athletic wear, and tables carry minimums — go for the lounge side of the room on weeknights.
Guest list > cover. Almost every room downtown has a free-before-11 list via DM or their promoter link. Two minutes of effort saves $20–40.
Last call is 2am. Kitchens close earlier — eat on King before 10pm or you're ending the night at a street dog cart (also a valid choice).
TTC after midnight: Line 1 runs past 1:30am weekdays; Blue Night buses cover the rest. Surge pricing on rideshare peaks at 2:15am — wait it out with a shawarma.
